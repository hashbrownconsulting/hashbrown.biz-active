# 02 · The problem

Most businesses can't stand up outbound on their own.

You built this on relationships.

Real revenue, won over dinners, site visits, and handshake deals. That works, until you're the bottleneck. Every new opportunity still needs you in the room, and there's one of you.

You've been held hostage before.

The industry standard is a freelancer running your outbound on accounts you don't control. Then the invoice dispute happens, and your pipeline disappears overnight. You've heard the stories.

You're getting outworked.

Your competitors have outbound sales systems firing every morning. You have a capable sales team waiting for the phone to ring.
