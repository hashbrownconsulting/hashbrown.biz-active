# Questions

Done-For-You is US$6,000 one-time, for the complete system built end to end, including domains, inboxes and sending infrastructure. It takes roughly eight weeks including warm-up.

Do-It-With-You consulting is US$200 an hour, or US$750 a month for four sessions. You run the system on tools you buy and own, which cost roughly US$100 to $300 a month, and we spec the stack before you spend anything.

Web development starts at US$1,000, depending on scope. A one-pager can ship in about a week, and bigger builds and monthly management are available.

Because the right scope depends on who you are reaching, your offer and what a booked call is worth. We quote them after the discovery call so the price matches the work.
