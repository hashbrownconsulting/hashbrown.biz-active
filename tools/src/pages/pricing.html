# Other ways to work together

A fixed fee, shared upside, or an introduction fee.

Lower fee plus commission

On some projects we take a lower fixed fee and a commission on the sales our work produces. This works when revenue is easy to attribute, such as deals booked and closed from a campaign we run. Terms are agreed in writing before work starts.

Revenue or profit share

Where we help build or run a commercial line with you, we can agree a share of revenue or profit, instead of or alongside a reduced fee. We take this on only where we can see how it will be measured.

Introduction and referral fees

On ordinary business services and software, we may agree a referral fee in writing with the party we are working for. It is disclosed to both sides, set before the introduction and reviewed by a lawyer. See introductions and partnerships.

We do not publish percentages, because they depend on deal size, margins and how much of the work we do. We will say plainly if a fixed fee is the better deal for you.

Commission and referral rules differ by industry and country. We take no introduction fees on investments, securities, financing, insurance, property, commodities, crypto assets, legal or medical services, hiring placements or anything tied to government decisions. We will not agree terms that your sector does not allow, and we have a lawyer review every arrangement first.
