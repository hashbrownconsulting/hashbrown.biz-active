# What you get

Manual outreach: every message written by a person.

For senior and high-value buyers, nothing beats a message that was clearly written for them. We build a hand-picked list around your ideal client, write each message one at a time, and handle the replies until they become booked calls.

  • Hand-picked prospect list built around your ideal client
  • Connection notes and messages written one at a time
  • Replies turned into booked calls, not left to go cold

Automated outreach: more reach, same standards.

When you need to reach more of the market, automated sequences do the repetitive work: targeted lists, tested messages, and a short follow-up. Pacing is set conservatively, and a person reviews every reply before anything reaches your team.

  • Targeted lists and tested message sequences
  • Pacing set conservatively, not pushed to the limit
  • A person reads every reply, and qualified ones go to your calendar

Which one is right for you?

It depends on who you are trying to reach and how many of them there are. Some set-ups use both: manual for a short list of key accounts, and automated for the wider market.

  • Manual suits senior, high-value buyers and small, well-defined lists
  • Automated suits larger markets where reach matters
  • We talk through the trade-offs on the discovery call before you decide
