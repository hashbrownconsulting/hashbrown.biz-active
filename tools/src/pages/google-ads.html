# What you get

Show up when a buyer is actually searching.

We build search campaigns around buying intent: the specific phrases people type when they are ready to talk to someone. We also block the searches that waste money, so spend goes on buyers rather than browsers.

  • Intent-led keywords, matched tightly to what you sell
  • Negative keywords that block jobs, freebies and the wrong audience
  • Ads written to match the search, not to impress
{{mk:serp_b2b}}{{mk:ads_keywords}}

A landing page that qualifies before it books.

Sending paid traffic to a generic homepage wastes most of it. We build or tighten a focused page that says who it is for, answers the obvious questions, and makes the next step a booking.

  • One page, one offer, one clear next step
  • A short form that qualifies without scaring people off
  • Built fast and mobile first, see web development

B2B and B2C are built differently.

B2B campaigns are built around buying intent and a page that qualifies the visit before it books a call. B2C campaigns are built around the offer, local intent and a fast path to booking or purchase. We build the one that fits your business.

  • B2B: longer decisions, so the goal is a qualified call
  • B2C: faster decisions, so the goal is a booking or a sale
  • Both judged on what a conversion is worth to you
{{mk:serp_b2c}}

Tracked to the booked call, not the click.

Clicks are easy to buy and easy to misread. We set up conversion tracking so you can see which searches lead to booked calls, bookings or sales, and put the budget where those come from.

  • Conversion tracking set up before campaigns go live
  • Reporting on cost per booked call, booking or sale
  • Budget moved towards what converts and away from what does not
