# What changes by industry

The channels stay the same. The detail does not.

The buyer

An engineer, a procurement lead and a founder read the same message differently. We write for the person who has to say yes.

The research

Some buyers search, some ask peers and some wait for a trade event. We build the channels that match how they actually look.

The rules

Some sectors have strict rules about what you can claim. We keep copy to what you can stand behind, and we do not give legal or regulatory advice.
