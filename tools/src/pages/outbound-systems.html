# What you get

Sending infrastructure that protects your main domain.

New domains and inboxes have no reputation, so we set up separate sending domains and warm them up on a schedule. Your main domain never takes a risk, and nothing goes out at volume until the infrastructure has earned the right.

  • Sending domains and inboxes registered in your name
  • SPF, DKIM and DMARC set up properly, not "mostly"
  • A warm-up ramp of about eight weeks, explained in how long cold email warm-up actually takes
{{mk:domains_card}}{{mk:warmup_card}}

Lead data matched to your ideal client.

A good message sent to the wrong list is still a bad campaign. We source and verify contacts against your ICP before anything is sent, so bounces and complaints do not damage the infrastructure you just built.

  • Verified contacts segmented by role, industry and fit
  • Lead sourcing built in-house, no per-lead agency markup
  • Lists exported and handed over with the rest of the system
{{mk:leads_table}}

Emails that read like a brochure, not a trick.

We treat a cold email as a digital brochure: short, brand-forward and honest about what it is, with one small ask. Sequences run across email and LinkedIn, timed and tested, and each follow-up adds something the last one did not.

  • Short messages with one clear, small ask
  • Email and LinkedIn touches in one timed sequence
  • No fake familiarity and no AI-written pretend research
{{mk:email_cold}}{{mk:sequence_timeline}}

Reply handling that ends in a booked call.

Replies are the point, and most DIY setups quietly fall apart here. Every reply is read, classified and routed: qualified conversations go to your sales team with the full thread, and everyone else stays in a structured follow-up sequence instead of falling through the cracks.

  • Interested, question, not now, referral and auto-reply handled differently
  • Qualified replies routed straight to your calendar
  • Follow-up that never reads as desperate, see lead follow-up: how fast, how often, when to stop

Documented, handed over, and yours.

Everything is built in your house: your domains, your inboxes, your workspace, your data. When the build ends the system stays, documented and handed over, so nobody can hold your pipeline hostage, including us.

  • Full documentation and credentials at handover
  • Qualified conversations tracked in your own pipeline
  • We stay available after the build
