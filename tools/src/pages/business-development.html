# What you get

Start with who you should be talking to.

Before any outreach goes out, we agree the ideal client, the roles that buy and what counts as a qualified conversation. This is the part most outsourced sales skips, and it decides whether everything after it works.

  • Ideal client and buyer roles written down
  • A shared definition of qualified, agreed with your sales team
  • Target account lists built against that definition

One system across email, LinkedIn and search.

Buyers meet you in more than one place. We run email and LinkedIn as one timed sequence, and support it with search and a landing page that makes the next step obvious.

  • Email and LinkedIn touches in one sequence
  • Search and landing pages for people already looking
  • Every reply classified and routed to the right person

Tracked to pipeline, and handed over.

You should always know what is working. We track each account from first touch to booked call to opportunity, report weekly in plain language, and document the whole process so your team can run it without us.

  • A pipeline view your sales team actually uses
  • Weekly reporting on booked calls and qualified replies
  • Documentation and credentials at handover

# How it works

Define. Run. Hand over.

01

Define

We agree the ideal client, the buyer roles, the offer and the definition of qualified. We tell you on the call if you are not ready for outbound yet.

02

Run

We build and run the first cycles across email, LinkedIn and search, and adjust weekly against booked calls instead of vanity metrics.

03

Hand over

The process, lists, sequences and tracking are documented and handed to your team. You own the accounts and the data.
