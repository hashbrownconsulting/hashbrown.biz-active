# What you get

Start with who is actually buying.

A data centre purchase rarely belongs to one person. Facilities, IT infrastructure, procurement and an executive sponsor each look at different things, and the one who replies is often not the one who signs. We map the buying group before any email is written, so the list and the message match the person reading.

  • Accounts grouped by what they buy and who signs
  • Contacts checked against the role, not just the company
  • One segment at a time, so results are readable
