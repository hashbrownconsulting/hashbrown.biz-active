# What you get

Define who the right counterparty is.

A good introduction starts with a clear profile: what they buy or sell, where they operate, who decides, and why they would care. We write that profile with you first, because it decides every list and message that follows.

  • A written counterparty profile you approve
  • Target lists built from that profile, not from a generic database
  • Decision makers identified by role, not guesswork
{{mk:leads_table}}

Approach them the way a person would.

Cold messages from a faceless sender get ignored. We approach counterparties with short, specific messages across email and LinkedIn, and we make it easy for them to say yes, no or point us to someone else.

  • Personal, honest outreach with one small ask
  • Replies read and classified, including referrals
  • Follow-up that adds something new each time
{{mk:li_chat_manual}}{{mk:email_thread}}

Qualified introductions, with context.

We hand over introductions, not a list of names. Each one comes with who they are, what they said and why we think there is a fit, so your team can pick up the conversation without starting from zero.

  • A pipeline view of every counterparty and where it stands
  • The full thread and notes with each introduction
  • You decide who to meet and what to agree

# How it works

Profile. Approach. Introduce.

01

Profile

We agree the counterparty profile, the offer and what a qualified introduction looks like. If outreach is the wrong route for your deal, we say so.

02

Approach

We build the lists and run outreach across email and LinkedIn, handling replies and adjusting weekly.

03

Introduce

Qualified counterparties are handed to your team with context. You take it from there and own the relationship.
