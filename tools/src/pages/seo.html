# What you get

Fix what stops search engines reading your site.

We start with a technical audit: titles, headings, canonicals, sitemap, structured data, internal links and speed. Most sites have a handful of fixable problems holding them back, and fixing them costs less than writing more content.

  • A written audit with the problems ranked by effort and impact
  • Fixes made on your site, or specified for your developer
  • Search Console and analytics set up to measure what changes

Choose keywords by buyer intent.

A keyword is only useful if the person searching it might buy. We group terms by intent, match each group to a page, and avoid building ten pages that compete for the same search. If we do not have volume data for a term, we say so instead of guessing.

  • Keywords grouped as commercial, comparison and informational
  • One target page per cluster, so pages do not cannibalise each other
  • Competitor results reviewed before we commit to a term

Content that compounds.

Good content answers a buyer's question better than the next result and links to the page that helps them act. We write it with the same care as everything else, and we keep it linked into the rest of the site. Our own insights follow this approach.

  • Pages and posts written to answer real questions
  • Internal links that pass relevance to your service pages
  • FAQ and structured data so AI search can quote you correctly
