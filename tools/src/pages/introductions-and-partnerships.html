# What you get

Introductions that are disclosed and written down.

When we introduce two businesses, the terms are agreed in writing before the introduction, and every party knows how we are paid. That protects you, the other side and us. If we cannot explain the arrangement in plain words to everyone involved, we do not do it.

  • Terms and definitions agreed up front
  • The fee is disclosed to every party involved
  • A fixed period, so the arrangement does not run on forever

Clear lines on what we will not do.

Introduction and commission rules differ by industry and country, and in some sectors they need a licence. We stay out of investments, securities and financing, and we will not take a fee that your sector does not allow. We do not take secret commissions.

  • No fees for introducing investments, securities or financing
  • Regulated sectors only where the law allows and you hold any licence
  • Public officials and state buyers only after a legal review

# Fit

What this is, and what it is not.

This is commercial partnership work: introducing businesses that want to trade with each other and agreeing how we are paid. It is not investment, legal or financial advice, and we do not act as your agent or sign on your behalf.

Rules on commission and referral fees vary by sector and country, and some arrangements need legal advice before they start. Get your own, and we will work to what your adviser confirms.

# How it works

Scope. Agree. Introduce.

01

Scope

A 60 minute call to agree who you want to meet, what a good deal looks like and whether a shared-upside structure makes sense at all.

02
