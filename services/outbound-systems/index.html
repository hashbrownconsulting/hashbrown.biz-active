# What you get

Sending infrastructure that protects your main domain.

New domains and inboxes have no reputation, so we set up separate sending domains and warm them up on a schedule. Your main domain never takes a risk, and nothing goes out at volume until the infrastructure has earned the right.

  • Sending domains and inboxes registered in your name
  • SPF, DKIM and DMARC set up properly, not "mostly"
  • A warm-up ramp of about eight weeks, explained in how long cold email warm-up actually takes

# How it works

Discover. Build. Hand over.

01

Discover

A 60 min call with the founders. We map your sales motion, your ticket size, and your capacity to receive leads. If you do not need this system, we will suggest other solutions.

02

Build or train

Done-For-You: we build the complete system, roughly eight weeks including warm-up. Do-It-With-You: we train your team to run it on tools you buy and own.

03

Hand over

Documentation, credentials, and a working system in your hands. You own it. We stay available.
